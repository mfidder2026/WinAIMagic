**Compare** measures every downloaded model the same way and puts the results side by side: the speed of writing and of prompt reading, and the use of VRAM and RAM.

- Start a measurement; the app loads each model in turn.
- **Export measurement**: save the results as a file. You choose where it goes.
- **Share on GitHub**: opens a form to share your measurement with other users. Nothing is sent automatically.

> Measure with as few other programs open as possible: they take VRAM, RAM and processor time. A game or a browser with many tabs can lower the result a lot.
